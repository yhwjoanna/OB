GROUND RULESCommon RulesP.03REV 2026.08
SAME RULES FOR EVERYONE

Common Rules: Apply to All Teams

Pass Criteria

Which planes pass inspection?

  • Folded as shown in the folding guide, with symmetric wings and firm creases
  • Thrown from the launch line, flies past the 3 m pass line without leaving the flight lane
  • Up to 2 test flights per plane; the best result counts
Paper Rules

How are materials issued?

  • Up to 10 sheets per pickup; repeat pickups allowed
  • Paper ruined in folding counts as used, with no penalty
  • Paper picked up but never used counts as waste: 50 points deducted per sheet
Inspection

Who judges a pass?

  • A neighboring team sends 1 inspector to check planes and measure distance
  • The instructor settles disputes
  • Test flights count within the 10 minutes; plan capacity accordingly

PAY PLANSPlan OverviewP.05REV 2026.08
FOUR PLANS + ONE ADD-ON

Five Variable Pay Plans (Confidential per Team)

PIECE-RATE

Piece-Rate Team

Each plane earns pay; more planes, more pay, plus a speed bonus. The most direct incentive.

MERIT-BASED

Merit Pay Team

Quantity and quality both count: extra pay for long flights, plus a milestone bonus for hitting the target.

BONUS

Bonus Team

Tiered target bonuses: pay only after crossing a threshold, with extra for going further.

PROFIT SHARING

Profit-Sharing Team

Class-wide output sets the bonus pool, shared by contribution; teams below the threshold receive nothing.

ESOP ADD-ON

Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP)

Add-on: buy shares before the start; dividends depend on class performance, with possible gains or losses.

Note: each plan has different rules, but task conditions are identical. After receiving the plan card, read the rules first, then decide the team's production strategy.

PLAN APiece-Rate TeamP.06REV 2026.08
PIECE-RATE PAY

Piece-Rate Team: Pay per Plane Produced

Rules

How are rewards calculated?

  • Base piece rate: 80 points per plane that passes inspection
  • Speed bonus: from the 11th plane on, 40 extra points per plane (120 points per plane)
  • Waste penalty: 50 points deducted per unused sheet

Based on a 5-person team; threshold = half the baseline output. For other team sizes, use the parameter calculator.

Example

Example: 20 planes completed

First 10 planes: 10 × 80 = 800 points
Planes 11-20: 10 × 120 = 1200 points
Total 2000 points

Total points = min(N, 10) × 80 + max(N − 10, 0) × 120 − waste × 50
Strategy tip: more planes, more pay; every plane counts. But planes that fail inspection earn nothing, so secure quality before pushing quantity.

PLAN BMerit Pay TeamP.07REV 2026.08
MERIT-BASED PAY

Merit Pay Team: Quantity and Quality Both Count

Rules

How are rewards calculated?

  • Base reward: 60 points per plane that passes inspection
  • Quality bonus: 40 extra points per plane flying beyond 5 m (bonus distance)
  • Milestone bonus: 400 extra points at 20 planes
  • Waste penalty: 50 points deducted per unused sheet
Example

Example: 20 planes completed, 10 of them beyond 5 m

Base: 20 × 60 = 1200 points
Quality: 10 × 40 = 400 points
Milestone: +400 points
Total 2000 points

Total points = N × 60 + planes beyond 5 m × 40 + 400 (if N ≥ 20) − waste × 50
Strategy tip: time is limited; should effort go to speed or distance? Is spending a few extra seconds pressing creases firm worth a quality bonus?

PLAN CBonus TeamP.08REV 2026.08
BONUS PAY

Bonus Team: Cross the Threshold, Earn the Bonus

Rules

Tiered target bonus (paid per team)

  • 12 planes: 800 points
  • 16 planes: 600 more points (1400 cumulative)
  • 20 planes: 600 more points (2000 cumulative)
  • 24 planes: 400 more points (2400 cumulative, cap)
  • Waste penalty: 50 points deducted per unused sheet
Example

Examples

11 planes: 0 points (first tier not reached)
20 planes: 2000 points
30 planes: 2400 points (no increase beyond 24 planes)

Total points = 800·(N≥12) + 600·(N≥16) + 600·(N≥20) + 400·(N≥24) − waste × 50
Strategy tip: bonuses are paid only when a threshold is crossed, so track progress closely. How many planes to the next threshold? Is there time for one more tier?

PLAN DProfit-Sharing TeamP.09REV 2026.08
PROFIT SHARING

Profit-Sharing Team: Class Earnings, Shared by Contribution

Rules

Bonus pool and allocation

  • Bonus pool = total class planes that pass inspection × 100 points
  • Allocation = bonus pool × (team output ÷ class output)
  • Participation threshold: team share must reach 12% (= 60% of the average share, with 5 teams); teams below it receive nothing, and that portion is not paid out
  • Extra bonus: 500 more points if team share reaches 30%
  • Waste penalty: 50 points deducted per unused sheet
Example

Example: team 20 planes, class 100 planes

Bonus pool: 100 × 100 = 10000 points
Team share: 20% (threshold met)
Allocation: 10000 × 20% = 2000 points

Total points = class total × 100 × (team output ÷ class total) + 500·(share≥30%) − waste × 50
Strategy tip: the more the class folds, the larger the bonus pool; but each team's payout depends on its share. Secure the participation threshold first, then push for 30% if capacity allows.

ADD-ONESOPP.10REV 2026.08
EMPLOYEE STOCK OWNERSHIP PLAN

Employee Stock Ownership Plan: a bet on the whole class

Rules

Add-on (open to all teams)

  • Decide before the game starts; no buying or selling shares once play begins
  • Each team has 200 points of starting capital and may buy 0-4 shares at 50 points each
  • Class reaches 80 planes: dividend of 75 points per share
  • Class reaches 100 planes: another 75 points per share
  • Class below 80 planes: shares worth zero, capital lost
Example

Example: buy all 4 shares (cost 200 points)

Class 100 planes: dividends 4 × 150 = 600 points, net gain 400 points
Class 85 planes: dividends 4 × 75 = 300 points, net gain 100 points
Class 75 planes: no dividends, net loss 200 points

Share payoff = shares × 75·(class≥80) + shares × 75·(class≥100) − shares × 50
Strategy tip: dividends depend on the whole class, not on one team's effort. There is only one decision, made before the game: place the bet, or keep the capital?
