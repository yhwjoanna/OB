Suggested Pacing for a Five-Minute Video

FINAL GROUP PROJECT ── 3 TO 5 MINUTES
[ 6 BLOCKS ]
≈ 300 SEC
Aim for five minutes and keep a small buffer, so the final cut lands within the 3 to 5 minute limit. The allocation below is a suggestion; groups may adjust it to their topic, but all six blocks must appear.
Opening 25 sOPENING
Open with the problem the concept solves, or one concrete scene. Member information does not take opening time; put it at the end of the video or in the Word file.
Definition 50 sDEFINITION
The academic definition plus one plain-language restatement. The definition must match the wording in the assigned literature.
Origin 75 sORIGIN
The scholars who proposed the concept, the year, the question they were answering at the time, and the later revisions or extensions.
Evidence 75 sEVIDENCE
At least one specific study: what was done and what was found. Numbers and sample sizes persuade more than adjectives.
Application 55 sAPPLICATION
One workplace situation showing how the concept changes a judgment or a practice. Members' own work experience is the best material.
Closing 20 sCLOSING
Answer the question raised in the opening and summarize the key points of the video.
Five minutes of narration is roughly 650 to 750 words in English; recordings made from a full script need noticeably fewer retakes
