Course Description

This course deepens students' understanding of organizational behavior theories, including self-management and leadership styles, in order to enhance employees' work effectiveness and quality of life within organizations. Drawing on academic theories and recent research findings, the course explains the mechanisms of human behavior in organizations and examines how corporate policies shape employees' psychological states and subsequent behavior.

Course Objectives

  • Introduce the fundamental concepts of organizational behavior and related issues in the contemporary business environment
  • Introduce academic theories at the individual, group, and organizational levels
  • Analyze the internal and external factors that influence individual behavior in business organizations
  • Connect organizational behavior concepts with real organizational contexts and personal experiences
  • Develop workplace communication skills, including clarity, persuasiveness, and listening

Academic Integrity and Generative AI

Individual assignments must be completed independently. Students may discuss how to approach an assignment but may not copy one another's work. Online sources and references must be cited with URLs and full references. Generative AI tools may be used reasonably for polishing and translating text, but assignments may not be written entirely by AI. The instructors will randomly discuss written work in class; students who cannot reasonably explain their own work will have their assignment and participation grades affected. See the NSYSU Guidelines on the Use of Generative AI Tools.
